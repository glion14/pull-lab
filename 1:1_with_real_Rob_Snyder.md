### PULL hypothesis

As a dedicated AI builder in a VC fund, I need to accomplish some AI project of my choice or something that I was tasked to do \- basically this is my role now, the reasons why to do this vary wildly in between funds. These projects are usually automations to save time or workflows that are supposed to give us unique advantage (alpha). When I start building I realized that the quality of my output is dependent on the data side of things. I tried:

* MCPs connected to our tools but those are unreliable, non-exhaustive,…  
* I tried expanding the tools we are already using, e.g. CRM, Notion… but those tools are not meant as data storages  
* I tried building my dedicated AI storage in the form of Airtable or something that I put together with Claude, and later on I realize that I spend more time managing the data storage than working on the actual thing I set out to accomplish

Most of the times I get the thing done but I end up something that is either far from ideal or incomplete. Or I worry if this will work long term but I have no idea of the alternatives.
