# Case study

The one situation we're trying to serve, written as PULL. Proposed by the weekly review and approved by the founder. Old versions stay in the changelog.

## Current version: v1 (approved 2026-11-27)

**Status:** hypothesis (nobody has bought or hit X yet)

**X (the success moment):** the buyer takes the costed fix plan to IC, IC signs off the first fix, and the operator starts it (inferred, no customer yet).

**Who:** a deal or asset lead at a mid-market private equity fund who personally owns an asset (or portfolio) that is behind business plan, with IC watching.

**PULL, in one sentence:**
> I believe a deal or asset lead at a mid-market private equity fund, who owns an asset that is behind business plan with IC watching, needs a costed fix plan for it and to start on it right now, because they have to show IC what's being done, and generic AI doesn't work because it isn't specific to one asset's P&L, and the operator or management team doesn't work because they don't track what's possible now and the lead has no time to do it themselves.

| | What it is | In their words |
|---|---|---|
| **P** · Project | Find the fixes for the asset that's behind plan, each with a cost and benefit the lead can approve or take to IC, and start on the first one. Revenue levers can come before cost. | "ideally they come back with a cost-benefit analysis that I can approve" ([Filip](../deals/filip-tomasik-activum-sg/deal.md), 11-18); "fair enough to focus on the revenue aspects as a first step" (Filip, 11-24) |
| **U** · Unavoidable | The asset is behind business plan, so IC is watching and the lead has to show what's being done. The IC date isn't in anyone's words yet. | "it's not performing in line with business plan. So, as soon as that happens, people listen" (Filip, 11-18); "we need to show to the investment committee there. we are doing all we can" (Filip, 11-18) |
| **L** · Generic AI (ChatGPT, Claude) | Not specific to one asset's P&L. | "it's just not specific enough" (Filip, 11-18) |
| **L** · The operator or management team | Knows the sector but doesn't track what's possible now; the last one was fired for falling behind. | "their full-time job is also not to screen the internet in terms of what tools and what intelligence is the realm of possible today" (Filip, 11-18) |
| **L** · Doing it themselves | No time once deals pick up, and they don't know the options. | "if I was working on acquiring 10 new assets, I don't have time" (Filip, 11-18) |
| **L** · Point vendors | Fine for one narrow issue; claims on revenue are hard to believe. | "it's kind of hard to believe unless the pitch is that, oh, we have a proprietary model" (Filip, 11-18) |
| **L** · Consultants | Charge to find the answer; nothing changes after the deck. From people outside the situation, so not yet proven for this Who. | "people want you to pay them to potentially come to a conclusion" ([Mikhail](../deals/mikhail/deal.md), 11-03); "nothing happened... It's like a quarter million." ([Ari](../deals/ari-salonen-infravia/deal.md), 10-15) |
| **Supply** | A paid two-week diagnostic on one asset: a costed fix list (revenue first), priced under the lead's own sign-off limit, delivered with the operator, ready to take to IC. | |

**What's common vs. specific:** common: one asset behind plan, a lead who owns it, IC watching. Specific: the sector (student housing for Filip), whether revenue or cost comes first, the lead's sign-off limit.

**Who is out:** funds with a captive AI or value creation team, people with no asset of their own (pre-close sponsors, holdco founders, LPs, advisers, service providers), large-cap funds.

**Proof:** none yet. Filip pulled (2026-11-18) but hasn't bought.

## Changelog (newest first)

### 2026-11-27 v1

- **What this version says:** the version above.
- **Why it changed:** first written version. Before this, the hypotheses lived in the 1:1 notes: v0 ("drive AI transformation to reduce costs as if it's existential"; consultants too slow and expensive) and an adjusted version (a costed fix plan for an asset behind plan, to show IC by a date). Across 13 first meetings (2026-10-15 to 11-24), v0's existential U didn't show up: cost cutting was "generally pretty high" priority at best (Aazam), and growth portfolios aren't cutting (Tomer). The only buyer who pulled, Filip, fits the adjusted version: an asset behind plan with IC watching, and he took it to an IC member himself. Gaps to test next: the IC date in the buyer's own words, consultants as an option the buyer has tried, and whether anyone pays.
