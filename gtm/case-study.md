# Case study

The one situation we're trying to serve, written as PULL. Proposed by the weekly review and approved by the founder. Old versions stay in the changelog.

## Current version: v2 (2026-11-27)

**Status:** hypothesis (nobody has bought or hit X yet)

**X (the success moment):** the first AI change is live in the company and shows in the P&L the lead reports to IC (inferred, no customer yet).

**Who:** a deal, asset or operating lead at a mid-market private equity fund who owns a portfolio company that is unprofitable or behind its business plan, with IC watching. Examples: a software company that has to go from hundreds of people to a fraction with AI (Rob, 1:1 session 2); a student housing portfolio behind plan with a new operator ([Filip](../deals/filip-tomasik-activum-sg/deal.md)).

**PULL, in one sentence:**
> I believe a deal or operating lead at a mid-market private equity fund, who owns a portfolio company that is unprofitable or behind its business plan, needs to rebuild that company's P&L with AI (cut the cost base, and lift revenue where that is the faster lever) right now, because the company is off plan and IC is watching what they do about it, and McKinsey or traditional consultants don't work because they're too slow, too expensive and stop at the deck, and generic AI and the management team don't work because they aren't specific to this company's P&L and don't know what is possible now.

| | What it is | In their words |
|---|---|---|
| **P** · Project | Rebuild one portfolio company's P&L with AI: find the changes with a cost and benefit for each, take them to IC, and start the first one. Cost is the core; revenue first where that's faster. | "ideally they come back with a cost-benefit analysis that I can approve" ([Filip](../deals/filip-tomasik-activum-sg/deal.md), 11-18); "fair enough to focus on the revenue aspects as a first step" (Filip, 11-24); "look at what the tasks are to be done and then build the agents that can do it and we can cut some costs" ([Aazam](../deals/aazam-vishram-osprey/deal.md), 11-04, project already running, no U) |
| **U** · Unavoidable | The company is off plan, so for the lead it is existential: IC is watching and they have to show what's being done. Where the company is on plan, U fails (see changelog). The IC date isn't in anyone's words yet. | "it's not performing in line with business plan. So, as soon as that happens, people listen" (Filip, 11-18); "we need to show to the investment committee there. we are doing all we can" (Filip, 11-18) |
| **L** · McKinsey, BCG, traditional consultants | Too expensive, and they don't implement: the deck lands and nothing changes. | "And they're too expensive." / "they're not going to get into the weeds of actually doing some of the work" (Aazam, 11-04); "nothing happened... It's like a quarter million." ([Ari](../deals/ari-salonen-infravia/deal.md), 10-15); "people want you to pay them to potentially come to a conclusion" ([Mikhail](../deals/mikhail/deal.md), 11-03) |
| **L** · Generic AI (ChatGPT, Claude) | Not specific to one company's P&L. | "it's just not specific enough" (Filip, 11-18) |
| **L** · The management team or operator | Knows the business, doesn't track what's possible now. | "their full-time job is also not to screen the internet in terms of what tools and what intelligence is the realm of possible today" (Filip, 11-18) |
| **L** · Doing it themselves | No time once deals pick up, and they don't know the options. | "if I was working on acquiring 10 new assets, I don't have time" (Filip, 11-18) |
| **Supply** | AI transformation as a service (Rob, 1:1 session 2), started small: a paid two-week diagnostic on one company that ends in a costed plan the lead can take to IC, priced under their own sign-off limit, then we build and run the first changes with the team, which is what consultants don't do. | |

**What's common vs. specific:** common: one portfolio company off plan, a lead who owns it, IC watching, consultants already judged too slow or expensive. Specific: the sector (software, real assets), whether cost or revenue comes first, the lead's sign-off limit.

**Who is out:** funds with a captive AI or value creation team ([James Cope / Hg](../deals/james-cope-hg/deal.md), [Steve Rooks / OTPP](../deals/steve-rooks-otpp/deal.md), [Tomer](../deals/tomer-amar/deal.md)), portfolios that are growing and on plan ([Tomer](../deals/tomer-amar/deal.md)), people with no company of their own yet ([Magnus](../deals/magnus-lossius/deal.md), [Max](../deals/maximilien-charbonnier-eccleston/deal.md), [Rob Savage](../deals/rob-savage/deal.md), [Ben Banks](../deals/ben-banks/deal.md)), large-cap funds.

**How to reach them:** they get pitched AI transformation constantly, so warm intros beat cold (Rob, 1:1 session 2). Ask every lead for the colleague or peer sitting on the company that's furthest off plan.

**Proof:** none yet. Filip pulled (2026-11-18) but hasn't bought.

## Changelog (newest first)

### 2026-11-27 v2

- **What this version says:** the version above. The core is back to Rob's hypothesis from 1:1 session 2 (P: AI transformation that cuts costs as if it's existential; U: it is existential; L: McKinsey and traditional consultants too slow and expensive; S: AI transformation as a service), with the call evidence added.
- **Why it changed:** the founder asked to realign with Rob's hypothesis. Checked against the 13 first meetings:
  - **L holds and is now the strongest part.** v1 had consultants as unproven; three buyers named them in their own words (Aazam on MBB, Ari's quarter million, Mikhail).
  - **U needed to be concrete.** "It is existential" in the abstract fails the delay test in the calls: where the company was fine, they waited ("generally pretty high" priority, Aazam; "nothing immediate", James; "everything is exploding", Tomer on a growing portfolio). The only U that showed up was a company behind plan with IC watching (Filip). v2 makes that the meaning of "existential".
  - **P is Rob's (rebuild the P&L with AI, cost at the core)**, widened to allow revenue first, because Filip chose revenue first on his asset.
  - **Who is widened** from v1's "asset" to any portfolio company off plan, so Rob's unprofitable software company and Filip's real assets fit the same case study.
  - **Supply is Rob's**, starting with the paid two-week diagnostic, with implementation named as the difference from consultants (Aazam).
- **Parked:** Rob's second hypothesis ("make sure we haven't spent all this money on AI credits and delivered nothing"). No buyer said it in 13 calls; not tested.
